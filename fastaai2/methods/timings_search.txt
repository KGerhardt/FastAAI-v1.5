Search phase: FastAAI 1 vs 1.5, same genomes, same machine, 8 threads.
Both figures are whole-process wall clock minus the ~0.22 s interpreter
start that both versions pay identically.

scale                   pairs         v1       v1.5   v1.5 pairs/s   speedup
120 genomes            14,400      1.15s     0.016s        922,339       74x
2,943 genomes       8,661,249     21.84s     1.587s      5,457,298       14x

Both v1 figures were measured with `db_query --in_memory --store_results`,
its fastest path. Peak RSS at 2,943 genomes: v1 1.01 GB, against a v1.5
index of 116 MB. Database on disk at that scale: v1 508 MB, v1.5 116 MB.

The two speedups are not in tension. At 120 genomes the pairwise work is
trivial and v1's per-query TEMP TABLE and INNER JOIN overhead is most of
the runtime, so the ratio measures fixed cost. At 2,943 genomes the work
dominates, and 14x is the figure that describes the engine.
